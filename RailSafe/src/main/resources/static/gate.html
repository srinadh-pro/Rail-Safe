What the labels mean

OPEN Reported clear. Not guaranteed.
CLOSED Reported closed. Never bypass.
UNKNOWN Missing, unverified or stale.

Source tells you where the status came from: authorised staff or an authorised sensor. Verification tells you whether staff reviewed it.

When a status is older than the configured stale window, RailSafe displays UNKNOWN even if the stored value was OPEN or CLOSED.
