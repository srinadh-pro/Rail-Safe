1. Find crossings near you

Share your location with permission and RailSafe lists nearby crossings, their distance and last reported status.

2. Plan a route

OSRM calculates the driving route. RailSafe checks which crossings fall within 150 m of that routing result and flags reported closures.

3. Get alerts

Status changes create in-app notifications. With browser permission, unread alerts can also appear as desktop notifications.

Safety comes before the app. RailSafe uses reported and verified crossing information. It is not connected to Indian Railways and cannot confirm a gate is open. A green “OPEN” label is still only a report. Never cross a closed barrier, never drive around a gate, and always follow railway signals and authorised staff.
