How alerts work

When an authorised staff member changes a gate to CLOSED, OPEN or UNKNOWN, RailSafe records the history and creates an alert for commuter accounts.

Each status event gets a unique deduplication key, so the same update is not delivered twice. Browser alerts require your permission and work best on localhost or HTTPS.

Notifications are based on reported and verified events. They are not live railway signals. Never rely on an alert to decide whether it is safe to cross.
